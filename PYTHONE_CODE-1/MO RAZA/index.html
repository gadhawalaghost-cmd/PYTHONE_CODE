Three screens, one shirt

How printing works here

Like a real screen-print run, your order passes through three simple stages — no design software needed on your end.

Screen 1

Pick your blank

Choose fabric colour and size — S, M, L, XL, XXL or 3XL — in 180 GSM combed cotton.

Screen 2

Choose the print

Go with one of our built-in designs, or upload your own photo, logo or artwork to press instead.

Screen 3

We press & ship

Your tee is printed, quality-checked and dispatched within 3–5 working days.
